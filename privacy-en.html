Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026 / Last updated: September 25, 2026

ToKiip does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. The question images and study records you save stay on your device. Your study materials are never transmitted outside your device. Information needed to verify a purchase is processed only when you use in-app purchases (see “5. In-App Purchases”).

1. Developer

ToKiip (“the App”) is developed and operated by an independent developer (“we”) as a study review app.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. No account is required.

Everything you save in the App — question images, question types and source names, review schedules and records, and your settings — is stored only in a database and files on your device, and is never sent to us or to any third party.

3. About Your Study Materials

The App saves only the area you select from a photo you take or choose. The full source page is discarded once the selected area has been saved, and is not kept on your device.

Saved images are never sent to an external server, cloud storage, image-recognition service, or generative AI service. No image is included in any information sent for bug reporting.

4. Camera and Photo Access

The App uses camera and photo library access so that you can add questions. Images obtained this way are processed only on your device.

Granting access is optional and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings. Without access, the feature for adding questions is unavailable.

The App does not use the microphone and does not request microphone access.

5. In-App Purchases

Purchases of the App's Pro features are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never have access to your payment details, such as credit card information.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to verify purchase status (receipt validation) and analyze purchase history. In this process, purchase history, receipt information, and an anonymous identifier are handled on RevenueCat's servers. This information is not linked to personal information such as your name or email address, and is not used for tracking across other apps or websites.

For details, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. External Transmission and Third Parties

  • Your images and study data are never sent to an external server. The App works offline.
  • Except for the purchase processing described above, we do not share your information with third parties.
  • We do not use app-usage analytics tools or advertising services. RevenueCat's purchase-history analytics are described above.
  • The only network communication the App performs is related to in-app purchases, as described above.

7. Lock Screen Display (Live Activities)

The App uses Live Activities to show today's review on your Lock Screen. The information shown is assembled on your device and is never transmitted externally. The App does not use standard push notifications.

Live Activities require your permission, which you can change at any time in iOS Settings or in the App's settings screen.

8. Printing and PDF Export

The printing and PDF sharing features export today's questions as a file when you choose to do so. Where that file is saved or shared is under your own control.

9. Backups

The App's data is stored in your device's documents area, so it is included in standard iOS device backups (iCloud or a computer). Restoring such a backup onto a new device also restores your saved questions and study records.

Those backups are made under your own Apple ID, and we have no access to their contents. Nothing is stored on any server operated by us.

10. Deleting Your Data

Because all data is stored only on your device, deleting the App removes all of it. You can also delete individual questions at any time within the App.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy in response to changes in law or to the App's features. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
